Alarmierung: the desktop app watches a folder for FF-Agent alarm mails (saved .eml, HTML body or XML) and offers each new alarm, or one opened by hand, on the Home screen as an Einsatz, opened with its Einsatznummer, Stichwort, address, Einsatzort on the Lagekarte, a first ETB entry and the ticked Einsatzmittel as Kräfte.
