The Stammdaten rail now separates the brigade's own Checklisten from Lagebuch's fixed
categories: the app's entries first, then a **CHECKLISTEN** heading and the lists the brigade
wrote itself, with «+ NEUE CHECKLISTE» pinned below them. Previously both appeared as identical
rows and nothing said which entries could be renamed or deleted. A brigade that keeps no
Checklisten sees the heading with a note instead of an empty box. The rail scrolls as one list
however many Checklisten it holds, and the button stays reachable.
