The Stammdaten take an Einsatznummer format such as `{A} 1.2 {JJMMTT} {NNNN}`, and the Einsatzdaten dialog of an incident without a number opens with the Einsatzart letter taken from the Stichwort and the date filled in, the caret on the gap for the Leitstelle's running number, and a non-blocking hint when a number does not match (#462).
