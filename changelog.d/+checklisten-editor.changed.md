Stammdaten can now create, rename, reorder and delete Checklisten. Each has its own entry in
the editor's left rail, with its name editable above its items and a «LÖSCHEN» button beside
it; «+ NEUE CHECKLISTE» sits under the rail. The new **Navigation** category orders the Einsatz
sidebar and switches entries on or off — modules and Checklisten in one list. The ETB's
checkbox is disabled: it is the legally relevant record every Systemmeldung lands in. Deleting
a Checkliste-Vorlage asks first and leaves Einsätze already started from it untouched.
