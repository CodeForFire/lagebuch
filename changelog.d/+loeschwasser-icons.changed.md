On the Lagekarte every Löschwasser-Entnahmestelle is now a flag standing on its exact spot, its glyph saying what it is (Unterflur-, Überflur- or Wandhydrant, Saugstelle, Brunnen, Zisterne, Löschteich) and its colour the flow class from OSM's `flow_rate` in five steps from under 400 to over 3.200 l/min, with the flow or, where none is recorded, the DN written on it, while further out it shrinks to a disc whose ring fills with the class.
