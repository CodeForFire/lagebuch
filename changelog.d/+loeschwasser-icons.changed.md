On the Lagekarte every Löschwasser-Entnahmestelle is now a flag standing on its exact spot, its glyph saying what it is (Unterflur-, Überflur- or Wandhydrant, Saugstelle, Brunnen, Zisterne, Löschteich), its colour the flow class from OSM's `flow_rate` in five steps from under 400 to over 3.200 l/min, and from zoom 18 also its DN, while further out it shrinks to a disc whose ring fills with the class.
