On the Lagekarte every Löschwasser-Entnahmestelle is now a flag standing on its exact spot, its glyph saying what it is (Unterflur-, Überflur- or Wandhydrant, Saugstelle, Brunnen, Zisterne, Löschteich) and its colour where the water comes from: red for a hydrant on the Trinkwassernetz, blue for a source independent of it. The flag says the DN, or a Löschwasserbehälter's volume, and close up also the Lage, the outlets by Storz size ("A, 2×B") and whether it stands on private ground; further out it shrinks to a dot in the same colour. Its card adds the outlets, the access and when a mapper last checked it on site.
